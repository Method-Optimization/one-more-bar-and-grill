Sponsorship & Donations

We've been part of Tabernacle since 2012, and we like helping out local schools, teams, fire companies and non-profits doing good work. If that's your group, tell us about it below.

  • Send your request at least a few weeks before your event, so we have time to plan for it.
  • We give priority to groups in and around Tabernacle and the surrounding towns.
  • Non-profits: attach a copy of your 501(c)(3) letter below. We can't release a donation without it.
  • We get more requests than we can say yes to, so we'll only be in touch about the ones we can help with.

Request form

A PDF or a photo of the letter is fine, up to 8MB. Not a registered non-profit? Leave this blank.

We read every request. Because of the number we get, we only reply to the ones we can help with.
