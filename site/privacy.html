Privacy & Cookies

Effective September 23, 2026

This website doesn't ask you for anything. There's no form, no login, no ordering system and no cookie banner, because there are no cookies to warn you about. What follows is the short, honest version of what happens when you load a page.

What we collect

Nothing you type

There is nothing on this site to fill in. To order food or ask us something, you call the bar on (609) 388-5386 or email wendyonemore@gmail.com — and then we know whatever you chose to tell us, the same as if you'd walked in.

A count of page views

We count how the site is doing so we know whether it's worth keeping up to date. A small script counts which pages get looked at, roughly how many visits that adds up to, which website sent you here (the site's name, like google.com — never the full address you came from), the country your connection is in, and whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer.

It uses no cookies, never records your IP address, and keeps nothing that could pick you out or follow you from one visit to the next — only daily totals, like "42 people looked at the menu on Friday". If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, it counts nothing at all. Method Optimization, who built and look after this site, run that counter for us.

The usual server records

Like every website, this one is delivered by a web host that keeps short-lived technical logs — your IP address, the page requested, the time. Our host uses them to serve pages and block attacks. We don't read them, and we don't combine them with anything else.

Who else sees anything

Loading any page on the web means your browser talks to whoever serves that page and anything on it. These are the only companies involved here, and what each one is for:

  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and filters malicious traffic. Their policy
  • Method Optimization — built and maintains the site, and runs the cookieless page-view count described above. Their policy
  • Sanity — stores the words, prices and photos on the site, so we can change the menu ourselves. Your browser loads our menu photos and the latest content from them, which means they see the request. Their policy
  • Google Fonts — serves the two typefaces the site is set in. Their policy
  • jsDelivr — serves one small script that makes scrolling smooth. Their policy

We don't run Google Analytics, advertising pixels, session recording, or a Facebook pixel. Our Facebook and Instagram links are ordinary links: nothing from those companies loads on this site, and they learn nothing unless you click through.

Texts about the specials

Texting onemore to 833-440-2766 is separate from this website. That's a text message service we use to send the specials to people who ask for them, and you're giving your number to that service, not to this site. Reply STOP to any of those messages to get no more, or ask us in the bar and we'll take you off.

What we never do

  • We don't sell, rent or trade your information — to anyone, for any price.
  • We don't set cookies on this site, or build a profile of you.
  • We don't take payments or store card details here.
  • We don't email you unless you asked us to.

Children

This is a bar's website and it isn't aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect anything about anyone under 13 — and since we collect nothing that identifies anyone, there's nothing to collect in the first place.

Questions, or asking us to delete something

Call (609) 388-5386 or email wendyonemore@gmail.com and a person here will answer. Depending on where you live you may have rights over information a business holds about you; ask and we'll honour them. For most people the honest answer will be that we hold nothing about you at all.

Changes
